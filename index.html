About Me

I am an ISPF TTHER Research Fellow / Postdoctoral Researcher at Edinburgh Napier University (ENU), United Kingdom, from April 2026 to March 2027. I am also a lecturer at the International College of Digital Innovation (ICDI) at Chiang Mai University. I hold a PhD in Pure Mathematics and am currently applying my expertise in advanced mathematics to the fields of data analytics and artificial intelligence.

Research Focus & Fellowship

My research bridges mathematics, computer science, and healthcare, focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics, and Digital Health.

During my fellowship at ENU, I am developing research on the application of artificial intelligence and data-driven technologies in healthcare. My interests span health data analytics, intelligent health monitoring, clinical and healthcare decision support, wearable and sensor-based technologies, and AI-enabled digital health solutions. My work explores how advanced computational methods can be used to improve the understanding, monitoring, prediction, and management of health and wellbeing.
